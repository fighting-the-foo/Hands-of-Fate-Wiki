## Level of sapience

Vampires are known to be among the most intelligent supernatural creatures, exhibiting the rare trait of above-human intelligence. Though no proper IQ tests have been administered, it has been hypothesized that the average vampire IQ may exceed the range testable by modern IQ tests. Vampires have been known to solve complex problems at a speed far exceeding what is exhibited in humans. The source of the exhibited intelligence of vampires is believed by experts to be due to a dynamic polymorphing of neural pathways though this hypothesis remains unconfirmed as polymorphing reverts upon death.

## Abilities

### Shape-shifting

Vampires are one of many shape shifting species. Vampires are able to take on animalistic qualities from a list of non-monstrous animals at will. this list is including but not limited to: Northern bats, gray wolves, Jaguars, true pythons, Egyptian vultures, and many others. Vampires achieve shape-shifting through [[polymorphing]] as apposed to via apoptosis. Vampires are capable of both full and partial body polymorphing however full body polymorphing depends on the current phase of the moon (see table below).

| moon phase      | full polymorph         |
| --------------- | ---------------------- |
| New Moon        | Gray Wolf              |
| Waxing Crescent | none                   |
| First Quarter   | Snake (species varies) |
| Waxing Gibbous  | none                   |
| Full Moon       | Northern Bat           |
| Waning Gibbous  | none                   |
| Last Quarter    | Egyptian Vulture       |
| Waning Crescent | none                   |

### Regeneration

Vampires, like many monsters, have a rate of cell regeneration that far exceeds that which is found in nature. Unless actively weakened, a vampire will regenerate cells at a pace that matches that of apoptosis. The rapid regeneration of cells allows vampires to survive their constant advanced use of polymorphing which would send most mortal beings into a state of potentially lethal shock.

### Enhanced durability

Vampires have a constant layer of magical defense generated around them which protects from most forms of physical harm.

## Diet

Despite famously exhibiting hematophagy; vampires are fully capable of consuming non-blood food sources. However, the amount of nutrients gained from this behavior is estimated to be low.

## Weaknesses

Vampires exhibit a range of weaknesses with varying levels of effectiveness.

### Allium flowers

Despite popular belief, vampires are not susceptible to harm from garlic cloves. They are however susceptible to an unknown compound found in the petals of the plant. When in physical contact with this compound, vampire flesh cannot regenerate at its usual rate. Additionally, the compound destabilizes the polymorphing magic surrounding them, causing the tissue to liquify and boil slightly.

### Holy magic

Vampires are very susceptible to harm from [[holy magic]] as it bypasses their enhanced durability and polymorphing capabilities.

### Sunlight

Sunlight both impairs a vampire's vision and causes direct harm to their skin. As nocturnal predators, vampires are not well adapted to seeing in bright sunlit environments which may cause disorientation and partial blindness. Sunlight exhibits some minor properties similar to the effects of [[holy magic]]; though it is unable to hinder cell growth as effectively. Vampires cannot be killed outright by sunlight under normal circumstances.

### Paranoia

Likely due to their advanced intelligence, vampires exhibit a fragile and paranoid psyche. When a vampire's paranoid tendencies are turned against them they have a common tendency to exhibit self destructive behaviors.

## Lifecycle

### Juvenile phase

The juvenile phase of a vampire's development tends to last two weeks to a month. During this phase, the vampire will shift uncontrollably between human and vampire forms. Juveniles do not exhibit all of the abilities or weaknesses of fully developed vampires; notably lacking the ability to fully polymorph, having a weaker form of a fully developed vampire's durability and lacking all weaknesses apart from allium flowers.

### Reproduction

Vampires reproduce via [[parasitic viral reproduction]] through bloodborne pathogen transmission, hematic ingestion or open wound contact with the pathogen, which can result in infection.
